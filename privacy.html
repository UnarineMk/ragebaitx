PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RageBaitX ("RageBaitX", "we", "us") handles information when you visit this website and when we use connected services to publish our own content. We keep the information we handle to a minimum.

1. Who we are

RageBaitX is an independent gaming entertainment and content brand that creates and publishes short-form gaming clips. You can contact us at contact@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.

2. Information we collect on this website

  • No accounts or forms. This website does not ask you to sign up, log in or submit personal information.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may automatically process basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • Cookies. This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Connected platform services (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook)

RageBaitX uses official platform APIs, including the TikTok API (Login Kit and Content Posting API), the YouTube Data API and the Instagram Graph API, solely to publish videos and captions to RageBaitX's own accounts through our internal publishing tool. When we connect our own accounts, the platforms provide:

  • basic profile information of the connected RageBaitX account (such as account ID, username and display name);
  • access tokens that let our tool upload and publish content to that account;
  • the IDs and links of content we publish, so we can keep a record of what was posted.

We do not use these services to collect information about other users, viewers or followers. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To publish RageBaitX content to our own social media accounts and keep an internal publishing log.
  • To respond if you contact us by email.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in encrypted credential storage provided by our automation platform and are only used by RageBaitX. We keep publishing records for as long as they are useful for running our channels, and delete tokens when an account is disconnected.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. To make a request, email contact@YOUR-DOMAIN.com. If a RageBaitX clip features you or your content and you want it removed, contact us and we will review it promptly.

7. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-party links

Our site links to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

RageBaitX is an independent fan and entertainment brand and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta.
